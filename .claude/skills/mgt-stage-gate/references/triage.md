# Lead triage: the Potential stage entry condition

Triage is a time allocation decision, not a tone decision. Tone belongs to house style. This decides how much effort a lead earns and how fast it should move.

## Read the commitment signal first

Strong signals, meaning invest and move to quote quickly:

- A named deadline, statutory or personal
- A specific situation with facts attached, not a general question
- A question that only makes sense if they are about to act ("how do I pay", "how long does it take", "what do you need from me")
- An AADE letter, a notice, a frozen account, a blocked transaction
- They have already been quoted elsewhere and are comparing
- They volunteer their AFM, their residency history, or their document status unprompted

Weak signals, meaning holding pattern:

- No date, or a date more than six months out
- General curiosity about how Greek tax works
- Asking only about price with no situation attached
- Third party asking on behalf of someone unnamed
- A question fully answered by the public site

Mixed is common. A distant date with a very specific situation is worth a real reply and a slow cadence. A tomorrow deadline with no facts needs one question, not a quote.

## The holding pattern

For weak signal leads, produce a short, warm, genuinely useful reply that answers what was asked, and nothing more. No pricing. No methodology. No document checklist. One question that would move them forward if they are serious.

Flag internally as low priority with a review date rather than entering the follow up cadence. Weak leads that never answer should age out quietly, not consume follow up slots.

The test for whether the holding reply is right: it should be useful enough that a serious lead replies, and light enough that an idle one costs nothing further.

## Quote unlocking questions

The only questions worth asking pre deposit are the ones without which a real quote cannot be issued. Ask the minimum set, not the intake form. The intake form is an Active stage instrument.

Typical unlockers by service shape:

- Residency work: which country are they tax resident in now, which country are they insured in, and what does the contract or income source look like
- Filing work: which tax years, resident or non resident for each, and what income types
- Registration work: where are they physically, what nationality, and do they already have any Greek credentials
- Anything involving a company or a business seat: what the actual arrangement is, since a short term lease cannot establish an έδρα

Three questions is usually the ceiling. More than that reads as an intake form and stalls the reply.

## Standard diagnostic flags to check silently

Do not raise these with the client pre deposit. Note them internally, because they change the shape of the quote.

- **Code 319 misclassification.** Recurring across multiple cases. A non resident flag applied to someone who meets the residency test produces erroneous balances. The registry correction must precede any return filing. If the facts suggest this, the quote needs a residency line, not just a filing line.
- **Legacy AFM data errors.** Wrong name, wrong parent name, or residency recorded at original issuance. Surfaces on non resident AFM and activation cases. Billable as a separate correction line.
- **Sequencing.** Registry correction before the associated return, always. Filing first locks in the wrong treatment.
- **Payment and revenue shape.** High revenue with very few invoices, or atypical payment flows, warrants enhanced due diligence before engagement or pricing. This is an appetite conversation with Chrysostomos, not a routine wholesale request.

## When to park immediately

- The request is outside what we do and there is no adjacent service
- They want a price match against a bulk filing shop. Hold the price, do not counter, park cleanly
- They are asking us to work from deceased credentials, or any other access route that cannot be used lawfully. Explain the lawful route once, then let them decide
- The lead is a partner or accountant candidate rather than a client. Different pipeline entirely

## What triage does not do

It does not set the price. It does not decide the payment structure. It does not promise a timeline. Those are Quoted stage decisions and they are Jim's call.
