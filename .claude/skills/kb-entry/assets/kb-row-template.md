## What this covers

The situation this entry applies to, and the situations it does not. Scope
stated up front stops the entry being applied to a case it was never written
for.

## The rule

One claim per paragraph, in plain English, with Greek terms explained on first
use, for example "AFM, the Greek tax number". State it as it applies to a
foreigner, since that is who every client is.

Where a figure, rate or threshold appears, attach its source inline so a later
reader can re-verify that one number without re-reading everything.

## Deadlines and dates

Give the scheduled date and say that live status is verified on AADE or myAADE
before it is stated to a client, because Greek deadlines are extended late in
the season more often than not. Label anything year specific as tax year and
filing year both, never a single year.

## Limits and exceptions

Where the rule stops applying, and who it does not help.

## What still needs partner confirmation

Anything on the R3 list this entry touches: multi country income, split year
residency, Article 5A, business structure choice, back year penalties, EFKA
interactions, ΣΑΔΦ treaty interpretation, or an imminent statutory deadline.
Phrase each as a question the partner can answer in one step.

Delete this section only when nothing here needs confirmation.
